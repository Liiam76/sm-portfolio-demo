# Product

## Register
brand

## What this is
A sample portfolio site for Amaka Oyelaran, a fictional Senior Social Media Manager in Lagos with 5 years of FMCG and consumer brand experience. It is a class demo on building a portfolio with Claude. Every person, brand, campaign and number is invented, and the site says so.

## Audience
Hiring managers, heads of marketing and brand managers at Lagos consumer brands, FMCG companies, fintechs and agencies. They skim on phones, often on mobile data. They want proof of results, platform fluency and a feel for the person's taste within 30 seconds.

## Goal
Get the visitor to download the CV or send an email about a full-time Senior Social Media Manager or Head of Social role.

## Brand voice
Direct, warm, numbers first. First person. Plain sentences. Nigerian consumer culture is referenced through the work and the copy, never through green-white-green palette clichés.

## Anti-references
Generic agency template, beige editorial-serif portfolio, SaaS hero-metric grids, stock photos of laptops and coffee.

## Constraints
Mobile first (Lagos traffic is mostly phones), light pages, no backend, accessible contrast, reduced-motion respected. No em dashes or banned vocabulary in copy.
